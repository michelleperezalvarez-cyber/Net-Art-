Entry 4: Childhood

Jan 24, 2024

Leaving has always been really easy for me
I chase the idea of a new place where I belong.
Found a picture from when I was a kid the first time I moved,
I was in the forth grade and that was the longest I've stayed anywhere.
I remember spending hours making up stories and thinking about the future,
I've never cared about what I was leaving behind.
