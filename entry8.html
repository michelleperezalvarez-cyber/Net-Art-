Entry 8: Staying

Apr 29, 2024

Been here a month and I've realized one thing.
I've been so focused on becoming a different person that
I haven't given myself time to understand the person I already am.
I've restarted my life so many times I don't know what was real and what wasn't,
I just know that I don't want to spend my whole life waiting for the next beginning.
